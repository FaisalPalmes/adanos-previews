No. 01 How a smashburger works

Ball, smash, crust.

What the smash in smashburger means, in three steps.

  1. 1

    Ball

    Every patty starts as a ball of halal Angus beef.

  2. 2

    Smash

    It goes on the griddle and gets pressed flat, so the whole patty meets the heat.

  3. 3

    Crust

    The edges cook thin and crisp. Cheese on, into the bun, and over the counter.
